Administration and security

Daily ownership of identity, access, retention policies and data protection for 2,500 people working across more than 75 countries. I tune the controls so security does not get in the way of work.

Entra ID, Conditional Access, Microsoft Purview and retention, SharePoint Advanced Management, Intune, SSO and OAuth integrations

Migrations to Microsoft 365

Change management, stakeholder engagement, end user training
